As a general strategy, work out what the remembered moment IS before you search. The request is a lossy recollection of ONE specific passage from a US congressional hearing transcript: names, dates and committees have been removed, so the passage must be recognised by its topic or policy area, the kind of exchange (trap question, confrontation, revelation, calm cross-examination, a witness cornered, a member losing patience), the witness's role and any phrase, number or rhetorical move the request describes. Name these concretely, then search for the passage from several directions: the policy topic in formal transcript language, the witness role, the rhetorical move, a paraphrase of what was likely said. The index is a dense semantic retriever: it matches meaning, not literal strings, and quotation marks have NO effect. Each hop, search a DIFFERENT angle rather than rewording the last one; judge whether an angle is working from the snippets themselves (the retriever's scores sit in a narrow high band even for unrelated text, so no fixed cutoff applies). Exactly one passage is correct, but you are building an ORDERED LIST scored by NDCG@10, so keep every plausible candidate, read the strongest ones in full, and spend your effort deciding which single passage matches the most clues.
